CANON MAGIC SYSTEM · SEVEN ARCANE TIERS

The Arcane System of Aulon

Magic as divine radiance given law: born from Aulla’s Light, structured by Aeynu, colored by the Seven Moons and commanded through sound.

THE ORIGIN OF MAGIC

Divine radiance given law

Magic in Aulon was not a mortal invention. The Atonis Novo explored the gods more deeply than any later civilization, translating Hymns into Ekhara and turning resonance into force, heat, light, movement and destruction.

No Sphere was more central to their arcane understanding than Aeynu, the Mystical Goddess of Magic and Arcane Law—the Equaliser. The Atonites spoke with her directly, recorded her Hymns and documented the laws she revealed.

Magic is Light containing Arcane Law.

The raw source is Aulla’s Light, eternally produced through the Bright Moon. By day it shines directly upon Aulon. At night the Seven Moons reflect it, each altering the radiance through its own resonance.

Magic is therefore neither fixed nor static. It changes with moon, season, realm and the divine resonance strongest upon the world.

THE BRIGHT MOON AND ARCANE LIGHT

Three divine contributions

The Bright Moon—understood by most mortals as the sun—is the eternal radiant source sustaining life and magic across Aulon.

KYNTUS

Solar Force

Fire, warmth and the physical power of the Bright Moon.

AULLA

Light and Life

Radiance that nourishes living creation.

AEYNU

Arcane Law

The structure that transforms radiance into magic.

All lawful magic within the Veil descends from luminous power, even when it manifests as darkness, blood, illusion, necromancy or beast sorcery. The Seven Moons act as divine filters, modifying its strength, behavior and temperament by season.

To cast without knowing the season is to fight the world’s current.

THE SEVEN TIERS OF MAGIC

From mortal technique to reality-command

The Atonites ordered magic by complexity, danger and proximity to divine authority. Tiers 1–5 belong broadly to mortal mages. Tier 6 approaches the divine threshold. Tier 7 is no longer ordinary magic.

ⅠFoundationⅡManipulationⅢExpressionⅣHigh ArcanaⅤHigh KnowledgeⅥDivine ThresholdⅦAbsolute Magic
Ⅰ
FOUNDATIONAL MAGIC

Tier 1

GREY MAGIC

Abjuration

  • Protection and barriers
  • Wards and negation
  • Resistance and defensive fields
  • Containment

The first serious school taught because it instills control, restraint and structural awareness. A mage who cannot defend safely cannot be trusted to cast dangerously.

Grey Magic resembles Emissary barrier arts, though true Emissary containment far exceeds it.

Ⅱ
INTERMEDIATE MANIPULATION

Tier 2

PURPLE MAGIC

Enchantment

  • Charm, calm and inspire
  • Confuse or alter perception
  • Strengthen resolve
  • Manipulate emotional reactions

Enchantment alters the Will behind the body. Ethical schools therefore restrict its use heavily.

ORANGE MAGIC

Transmutation

  • Harden or soften materials
  • Alter metals and density
  • Modify physical traits
  • Convert one state into another

Used by artisans, alchemists, builders, engineers and experimental magi. At its highest levels, it approaches rewriting physical nature.

BROWN MAGIC

Evocation

  • Firebolts and shockwaves
  • Force blasts and stone strikes
  • Elemental bursts
  • Magical artillery

Direct magical output and the most familiar school of battlefield spellcraft. Its results are immediate, visible and destructive.

Ⅲ
ADVANCED EXPRESSION

Tier 3

Tier 3 requires greater imagination, stability and command of Arcane Law.

YELLOW MAGIC

Conjuration

  • Summon creatures
  • Call tools and weapons
  • Create temporary constructs
  • Draw objects across space
  • Manifest magical servants
  • Invoke through bounded agreements

Depends upon contracts, circles, names and spatial precision. Failure can call the wrong being, break containment or open dangerous pathways.

PINK MAGIC

Illusion

  • False images and disguises
  • Phantom sounds
  • Emotional impressions
  • False terrain
  • Dreamlike overlays
  • Sensory misdirection

Illusion changes perception rather than reality. At high levels, that distinction becomes dangerously thin. It flourishes beneath the Azure Moon.

Ⅳ
SPECIALIZED HIGH ARCANA

Tier 4

Tier 4 begins high specialization. Its schools are powerful, dangerous and often culturally restricted.

BLUE MAGIC

Astral Arcana

  • Astral projection
  • Soul-adjacent perception
  • Dream walking
  • Mental extension
  • Spatial awareness
  • Star-aligned rituals
  • Mapping unseen planes

The caster must perceive higher layers without losing the self. Azure Moon saturation greatly strengthens this school.

RED MAGIC

Blood Arts

  • Empower the body
  • Seal wounds
  • Manipulate blood
  • Track bloodlines
  • Fuel sacrificial rites
  • Bind life force
  • Strengthen or curse flesh

Its power always demands cost, reminding every practitioner that life can be spent. It peaks beneath the Crimson Moon.

GREEN MAGIC

Beast Sorcery

  • Communicate with beasts
  • Strengthen companions
  • Heighten senses
  • Invoke instinct
  • Transform the body
  • Command lesser creatures
  • Draw upon Yggra

Not animal control, but instinct studied as magic. It peaks in the Season of Mooning.

Ⅴ
FORBIDDEN AND HIGH KNOWLEDGE

Tier 5

Tier 5 stands near the limits of mortal tolerance. These schools are regulated, feared and restricted for their proximity to death, fate, prophecy and hidden truth.

BLACK MAGIC

Necromancy

  • Raise and animate corpses
  • Communicate with spirits
  • Manipulate death energy
  • Preserve bodies
  • Bind ghosts
  • Study soul remnants

Necromancy belongs to lawful Aulon and is not inherently Blighted. Its danger comes from approaching Omongo’s domain and violating the proper passage of souls. It peaks during Preservation.

WHITE MAGIC

Divination and Clairvoyance

  • See possible futures
  • Locate hidden things
  • Reveal lies
  • Read omens
  • Receive visions
  • Interpret divine signs
  • Observe distant places

White Magic is not simply holy magic. It does not necessarily heal or purify. It reveals. Illumination exposes what is hidden and strengthens its sight.

Ⅵ
DIVINE THRESHOLD MAGIC

Tier 6

Rarely taught openly and generally inaccessible to ordinary magi. Its practitioners stand close to the divine, are deeply initiated—or dangerously corrupted.

Both Tier 6 branches intensify during the Cyclical Eclipse. As ordinary magic weakens, the world becomes vulnerable and distant threshold powers grow easier to access.

LAWFUL SYMPHONY

Sacred Magic

  • Elemental summoning
  • Rune engravement
  • Sacred contracts
  • Divine inscriptions
  • Stabilized ritual geometry
  • Elemental binding

Elemental Summoning calls beings from the Elemental Planes through contract. Rune Engravement carves lasting magical law into objects and places. Discipline, pure intention and divine-law expertise are essential.

SACRILEGIOUS MAGIC

Moonless Magic

  • Eldritch Magic
  • Sigils and Palinoian marks
  • Blight-based casting
  • Forbidden invocations
  • Corrupted resonance

Functions without the Seven Moons’ permission, drawing from Ena’s Reservoir and the Cradle of Sin. The Eclipse blocks Aulla’s Light, thins the Veil and lets the Outside press closer.

Moonless Magic is powerful because it violates the normal order. Every use deepens separation from the Symphony.

Ⅶ
ABSOLUTE MAGIC · REALITY-COMMAND

Tier 7

Many scholars refuse to call Tier 7 magic. Lower tiers manipulate Arcane Law from within. Tier 7 addresses reality at its foundation.

PRIMORDIAL SPEECH

Ekhara and Hymns

Ekhara compresses divine sound into mortal speech. Hymns are the original sounds of the Spheres, Grade X phenomena that cannot be possessed—only heard, invoked, translated or survived.

OUTWARD DECREE

RULE

A Lancer’s complete resonance manifests a Grade 9 Primordial Lance and imposes a temporary law upon the world. It is not a spell. It is a decree.

ABSOLUTE BOUNDARY

END

Emissaries create nullifying barriers and geometric containment. Where RULE imposes, END denies. Where RULE changes, END preserves.

Together, RULE and END are the two greatest surviving peaks of Primordial Speech in the Third Age.

SEASONAL ARCANE ENHANCEMENT

Magic is not only learned. It is timed.

Because arcane power flows through moon-reflected light, each season strengthens different schools. Institutions schedule examinations, rituals, wars, summonings, expeditions and experiments around these currents.

THE PHRONTISTERIES OF MAGIC

Temples of study

The Atonites organized formal arcane scholarship into great Phrontisteries—academies of thought, resonance, Arcane Law and magical discipline. Three were built at Aulon’s extreme ends, each preserving distinct traditions and portions of Atonite knowledge.

They were not merely schools. To study magic was to study the relationship among light, law, language, soul and the gods.

The Central Academy of High Ostra

By the Third Age, the Academy within the Gleaming City is Aulon’s greatest surviving center of arcane learning. It teaches recognized schools, preserves Atonite fragments and maintains strict relationships with Aulla’s theological authorities.

OPEN CURRICULUM

Tiers 1–5

Recognized mortal schools taught through doctrine, practice and controlled specialization.

THE DIVINE EDGE

Tiers 6–7

Tier 6 requires divine proximity, sacred authorization or forbidden access. Tier 7 belongs chiefly to Lancers, Emissaries and surviving Ekhara knowledge.

High Ostra teaches knowledge to the edge of divine danger—but not beyond it.

BOOK-BOUND

The page before the spell

Students of texts, theory, memorization and guided exercises. They understand principles but cannot yet cast reliably under pressure.

MAGI INITIATUS

Beginning practitioner

Legitimate but inexperienced mages commanding Tier 1 and beginning Tier 2. Most guild-supported casters begin here.

ADEPTUS ARCANUS

Arcane vocation

Reliable command of Tiers 1–2 with early Tier 3 study. Valuable in battle, research, healing, warding, construction and support.

MAGUS MAJORA

Regional authority

Experienced Tier 3 casters serving courts, expeditions, military units, temples and independent research circles.

MAGUS MAXIMO

Trusted with danger

Elite Tier 4 masters with specialized Tier 5 knowledge, capable of influencing battles, politics, academies and restricted research.

SAGE PRIMA

Living archive

Near-legendary masters of Tier 5 with profound Tier 6 theory. Their understanding may shape generations of magi.

AUTOCH

Fluency in one color

Recognition of perfected understanding rather than simple social power. Autochs may exist at different tiers; within the chosen discipline, casting is instinctive, efficient and complete.

To become Autoch is to cease imitating magic. It is to speak fluently in its chosen color.

MAGIC, EKHARA AND LANCERS

Three levels of metaphysical authority

THE MAGE

Uses Arcane Law

Casts within the system through learned schools, technique and structured radiant power.

THE EKHARA SPEAKER

Speaks to the system

Invokes translated divine sound with Will, addressing magic closer to its foundations.

THE LANCER

Channels the origin

Becomes a vessel for the Sphere whose Hymns helped create the laws themselves.

A Sage Prima may understand magic more intellectually than a young Lancer, but the Lancer carries direct divine resonance.

Knowledge can challenge power. But it cannot always equal origin.

THE ARCANE DOCTRINE

The visible grammar of creation

Magic is Light given form by Law, altered by the Moons, and commanded by Sound.

Aulla provides the radiance. Aeynu gives it structure. The Seven Moons color its expression. The seasons alter its strength. Ekhara grants it language. Hymns reveal its origin. RULE and END demonstrate its highest surviving authority.

Magic in Aulon is not merely a tool.

It is the visible grammar of creation.
