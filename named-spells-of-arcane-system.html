Named Spells of the Arcane System

Sixty remembered techniques of mortal and near-divine spellcraft, catalogued from the First Walls of Tier 1 to the Sacred and Moonless arts of Tier 6.

THE LIVING LANGUAGE OF MAGIC

Spells remembered by name

Schools and tiers give magic its structure. Named spells give it identity in history. Throughout Aulon, these arts are recognized by magi, adventurers, soldiers, scholars and common folk—some as academy foundations, others as feared battlefield techniques, and a rare few as names spoken only in restricted halls.

They do not belong to Tier 7, the realm of Primordial Speech, Hymns, RULE and END. These are the practiced techniques of mortal and near-divine spellcraft from Tiers 1 through 6.

GREY MAGIC · ABJURATION

The First Walls

The foundations of defensive spellcraft. A mage who cannot defend, stabilize or contain magic is not yet worthy to cast destructively.

FOUNDATIONAL ABJURATION

Grey Magic

MINOR BARRIER

Eil Veyra

Blocks light attacks

Forms a brief translucent shield against weak strikes, low-grade projectiles and minor force. A boundary is the beginning of control.

RESONANCE DAMPENING

Na Thren

Reduces magical output

Weakens local resonance, hindering spells and ongoing effects. Common in duels, classrooms, training halls and containment chambers.

GUARDIAN CLOAK

Durada Veil

Protective body aura

A close-fitting field that moves with the caster, guarding against glancing blows, weak curses and environmental hazards.

SOUL STABILIZER

Nullis Aeth

Prevents minor corruption

Stabilizes the soul’s outer layers against spiritual contamination. It cannot reverse deep Blight or save the Palinoian-marked.

WEIGHT ANCHOR

Kharon Brace

Prevents displacement

Anchors body and spiritual Weight against weak force, knockback, shoving and minor displacement magic.

REFLECTIVE GUARD

Eil Prismata

Reflects weak spells

An angled shimmering shield that redirects low-tier magic. Many students meet their first offensive spell twice.

SPELL INTERFERENCE

Thren Lock

Interrupts low-tier casting

Disrupts a spell as it forms. Poor timing does nothing; perfect timing silences an opponent at the most humiliating moment.

SHARED PROTECTION

Durada Nexus

Links allied barriers

Lets multiple shields reinforce one another and distributes damage across linked protections. Favored by military magi.

LIGHTNING NEGATION

Na Vash

Absorbs electrical attacks

Disperses lightning before it overwhelms the target. Essential against Raiju, lightning magi and servants of Xhin.

BASIC FORTIFICATION

Grey Bastion

Stationary defensive wall

Slower than Eil Veyra but sturdier, ideal for corridors, wounded allies and delaying monsters during retreat.

THE REMEMBERED ARTS OF AULON

Techniques beneath primordial authority

Named spells are the working vocabulary of magi—the words soldiers recognize, apprentices memorize and survivors learn to fear.

A spell earns a name when history remembers what it did.

Above them remain the Hymns, RULE and END: not techniques within magic, but commands addressed to the law beneath it.
