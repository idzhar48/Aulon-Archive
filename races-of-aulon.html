The Races of Aulon

A resonant taxonomy of the first people, modern mortals, elemental beings, celestial entities, daemons, giants and every created or ascended form of life.

THE ATONIS CLASSIFICATION

Race is not merely biological. It is resonant.

The peoples and beings of Aulon are commonly classified under the ancient term Atonis, meaning those whose existence descends from, reflects, or was shaped within the creative resonance of Aton.

This classification includes far more than ordinary mortal races. Every people possesses a relationship to the Spheres, the soul, magic and the ancient Symphony through which creation first took form.

At the head of all known races stand the first people: the Atonis Novo.

THE FIRST PEOPLE · THE ATONITES

Atonis Novo

The Atonites were the first civilization of Aulon and the earliest people born beneath the direct love and guidance of the Ephims. They were not merely an ancient race, but the first great choir of creation made flesh.

Unlike later civilizations that worshipped the gods from a distance, the Atonites lived in close communion with their creators. The songs of the Ephims shaped culture, law, science, art, magic and daily life. They understood that the soul possessed Tune, resonance could invite blessing, and sound—properly understood—could become power.

Appearance and lifespan

Atonites possessed purple skin that deepened with age. Young Atonites bore lighter violet complexions; elders developed rich, dark-purple skin that became increasingly resilient to the elements. Age did not weaken them. It refined them.

They suffered neither debilitating illness, natural degeneration nor the biological decline common to later mortals. Their children reached functional adulthood within approximately ten years. In time, they unlocked immortality: not crude preservation, but a harmonized state achieved through soul resonance, Hymns and the blessings of the Ephims.

A civilization of song

Choirs were religious rites, civic institutions, magical engines, military formations, schools and philosophical expressions. To sing correctly was to align oneself with creation. To sing together was to strengthen society. To fall out of tune was a spiritual danger.

Technology of the Atonites

Their technological advancement remains unmatched. Through mastery of primordial Hymns, they converted sound into heat, light, pressure, motion, impact and other usable forces.

Their famous firearms used no gunpowder or mundane combustion. They channeled structured sound into destructive output, becoming instrument, weapon and magical conduit at once.

  • Resonance firearms
  • Choir-powered artillery
  • Harmonic engines
  • Sound barriers
  • Hymnic amplifiers
  • Sonic transportation
  • Resonance healing chambers
  • Material-altering instruments

To later civilizations, this technology is indistinguishable from divine relic-work. To the Atonites, it was simply the natural result of understanding creation properly.

Relationship with the Ephims

The Atonites loved their creators deeply and were loved in return. They learned from the Ephims, sang to them, studied their sounds and built civilization around their blessings. This closeness revealed Soul Weight, Soul Tune, Resonance, Hymns, divine blessing, the nature of the Spheres and the necessity of harmony.

It made them uniquely powerful—and uniquely dangerous. No later mortal race would stand quite so close to the original music of the world.

HUMANS · THE SUPREME RACE

Atonis Tatura

Humans are called the Supreme Race not because they are strongest, longest-lived or most magical by default, but because of their adaptability, variability and extraordinary range of potential.

Their flexible souls may rise as heroes, Adamantite adventurers, Lancers, saints, scholars, tyrants, Blighted vessels or world-changing figures. No other mortal race displays such extremes of destiny.

EUPHONIUS HUMANS · DESCANTS

Blessed Vessels

Born with unusually strong Soul Weight and harmonious Tune. They often show great potential early and may become powerful adventurers, exceptional mages, spiritual leaders or Lancer candidates. Their natural resonance draws the attention of Spheres, guilds, temples, nobles and hidden divine agents.

MANUSCRIPTS · CANVASES

Blanks

Ordinary humans with neutral or undeveloped potential. They are not weak; they are unwritten. Training, hardship, education, artifacts, faith and will determine their growth. Many great adventurers begin as Blanks and carve power into themselves.

TACETS · TACITURNS

Reticents

Humans whose souls hold little natural growth potential. Their Tune is quiet, muted or difficult to develop. Some seek dangerous forced power through the rebranding of the soul by Hymns—rare, unstable and morally fraught. Not all souls begin with equal resonance.

A soul need not be born complete to become great.

K
DWARVES · THE RESILIENT RACE

Atonis Kyntu

Dwarves embody endurance, craft, invention, mineral wealth, forge-work and survival beneath harsh conditions. Though named in relation to Kyntus, Sphere of Fire and Warmth, they also hold deep bonds with earth, metal, stone and subterranean life.

  • Mountain · Gold Dwarves
  • Deep · Silver Dwarves
  • Hill · Bronze Dwarves
  • Volcanic · Molten Dwarves
  • Surface · Steel Dwarves

Mountain Dwarves are often the richest and most inventive, commanding vast mineral wealth and advanced engineering. Deep Dwarves master subterranean survival and hidden mining networks. Hill Dwarves commonly become traders, builders and frontier craftsmen.

Volcanic Dwarves forge materials other races cannot approach. Surface Dwarves adapt most readily to mixed civilizations, appearing throughout guilds, kingdoms, armies and trade cities.

Dwarves are not merely craftsmen. They are endurance given culture.

M
ELVES · THE MAGICAL RACE

Atonis Mulfaheem

Elves are especially sensitive to mana, resonance and the subtle movements of magical law. Their bodies and souls are naturally suited to arcane practice, making them among Aulon’s most refined spellcasters.

  • High Elves
  • Nature · Woodland Elves
  • Sea Elves
  • Ashen Elves
  • Gale Elves
  • Dark Elves

High Elves command the greatest raw magical power and often dominate arcane institutions. Woodland Elves follow forests, nature magic and Evas’s living rhythms. Sea Elves dwell near or beneath the waters, aligned with Traeyla’s flux and mist.

Ashen Elves hold affinities for fire, ruin, ash and renewal after destruction. Gale Elves align with air, speed, movement and wind-borne magic. Dark Elves are associated with shadow, secrecy, subterranean civilizations and obscure magical traditions.

Elves are typically long-lived and culturally inclined toward artistry, memory, spellcraft and elegance.

Where humans are adaptable, elves are refined.

MI
SEA DWELLERS · THOSE WITHIN THE SHORES

Atonis Mirima

The Sea Dwellers are bound to oceans, reefs, trenches, tides, rivers and coastal civilizations. Many honor Traeyla, Sphere of Water and Mist, though their cultures differ dramatically by habitat and ancestry.

  • Tritons
  • Fishmen
  • Sharkmen
  • Whalemen
  • Turtle Men
  • Kelp Men
  • Coral Men

Tritons are often the most humanoid and politically organized. Fishmen, Sharkmen and Whalemen vary widely in physique, temperament and culture according to their marine traits. Turtle Men are renowned for endurance, wisdom and defensive traditions.

Kelp Men and Coral Men blur the boundaries among animal, plant and reef-based life, forming societies difficult for land-dwellers to fully understand.

Civilization in Aulon does not belong only to the land. Entire histories unfold beneath the waves.

V
ORCS · THE SURVIVAL RACE

Atonis Voraco

Orcs are a powerful, highly adaptive people shaped by hostile environments, physical dominance and survival instinct. They are not uniform; each lineage bears the mark of the land that forged it.

  • High Orcs · Surface
  • Beige Orcs · Deserts
  • Black Orcs · Wastelands
  • Grey Orcs · Mountains
  • Azure Orcs · Arctic
  • Blood Orcs · Rainforests

High Orcs are considered the strongest and hold great social prestige. Desert Orcs master heat, thirst and open-terrain warfare. Black Orcs endure ruined wastelands. Grey Orcs excel in highland survival, stone warfare and mountain raiding.

Azure Orcs thrive amid frozen hunts and arctic conditions. Blood Orcs are feared jungle warriors renowned for ambush, ferocity and adaptation to dense living terrain.

Orcs embody the law that survival itself can become civilization.

S
BEASTMEN · THE UNLEASHED RACE

Atonis Sagyr

Beastmen are humanoid peoples bearing the traits of animals, primal spirits or ancient beast lineages. Their existence reflects the influence of Yggra, Mother Goddess of Beasts and the Primeval Animus.

  • Mammal-humanoids
  • Avian-humanoids
  • Amphibian-humanoids
  • Lizardmen
  • Dragon-Born

Mammal Beastmen include wolfkin, lionfolk, bearfolk, deerfolk, foxfolk and countless others. Avians may possess wings, talons, feathers or heightened perception. Amphibians flourish in wetlands, swamps, rivers and rain-soaked lands.

Lizardmen are hardy reptilian peoples of heat, marshes, deserts and ancient ruins. Dragon-Born are among the most revered and feared, carrying distant draconic traits without being true dragons.

Many Sagyr societies follow instinct, clan, territorial bonds and profound relationships with the natural world.

They are not lesser for being closer to beasts. Instinct is one of creation’s oldest forms of wisdom.

X
NATUREFOLK · XURI’S BREED

Atonis Xuria

Naturefolk are peoples whose bodies and societies intertwine with ecosystems, metamorphosis, spores, colonies and subtle natural magic. Unlike elves or Beastmen, they are often less mammalian and less constrained by familiar humanoid forms.

  • Insect-humanoids
  • Arachnid-humanoids
  • Fairies
  • Fungal-humanoids

Insect-humanoids form hive societies, artisan colonies, warrior swarms and specialized castes. Arachnid-humanoids are associated with weaving, venom, patience, ambush and hidden architecture. Fairies are small but potent beings of magic, mischief, nature, illusion and miniature courts.

Fungal-humanoids may possess collective memory, spore communication and intimate bonds with underground or magically saturated environments.

Some think as individuals. Some think as colonies. Some think as forests.

P
THE BLOOD OF GODS · DIVINE CLASSIFICATION

Atonis Paragonium

The Paragonium are beings of extraordinary origin: the True Dragons, the TorchBearer and, most commonly, the Pure Elementals. Their existence derives from direct elemental, divine or primordial force.

  • Dryads · Nature
  • Ifreets · Fire
  • Undines · Water
  • Gnomes · Earth
  • Sylphs · Wind
  • Raijus · Lightning
  • Boreas · Ice
  • Phantoms · Dark
  • Luminas · Light
  • Celestials · Space

Their origins are not uniform. Some are Pure Elementals born from the Elemental Planes. Some are ascended souls refined into higher elemental forms. Some are divine servants shaped directly by the Spheres. Phantoms may be bound to deathly servitude and lack free will.

The Atonis Paragonium are not merely races. They are forces of creation given form.

THE SOUL BEYOND DEATH

Zenithum and Nadirim

ATONIS ZENITHUM · BEINGS OF HEAVEN

Angels of Zenith

Post-mortal beings formed from souls that passed beyond the Gate of Eternity and were reinstituted by Aulla’s Light. Their forms express devotion, duty, virtue, resonance and divine function.

  • Reaper Angels
  • Cupid Angels
  • Warrior Angels
  • Adamantite Golems · Guardians

Reapers protect transitions and sacred thresholds from daemons. Cupids arise from souls aligned with Zayra’s love, purity and passion. Warriors serve celestial armies. Adamantite Guardians are nigh-unbreakable defenders, often made from souls that died protecting those they loved.

The Zenithum are not born. They are fulfilled.

ATONIS NADIRIM · INHABITANTS OF NADIR

Daemons of Nadir

Daemons arise when souls are refined beneath Yggra’s overwhelming emotional and beastly aura. They are not Blighted creatures, servants of Ena or punishments. They are True Shapes.

  • Jinns
  • Succubi
  • Pure Demons
  • Demon Beasts

Jinns are often bound to vessels. Succubi embody desire, temptation, emotional hunger and intimate manipulation. Pure Demons are direct daemonic manifestations; Demon Beasts take primal forms shaped by emotion and combat.

Unlike Omongo’s undead, many retain free will. Unlike Blighted creatures, they remain part of the ordained soul cycle. Nadir is terrifying, but it is still within the order of Aulon.

NU
GIANTS · THE COLOSSAL ONES

Atonis Nuga

Giants are ancient beings of immense size, physical force, environmental adaptation and elemental resonance. They inhabit places too vast or hostile for smaller peoples.

  • True Giants
  • Forest Giants
  • Mountain Giants
  • Storm Giants
  • Frost Giants
  • Flame Giants
  • Cloud Giants

True Giants are the purest or oldest kind. Forest Giants walk ancient woods and colossal groves. Mountain Giants inhabit peaks, cliffs and stone realms. Storm Giants embody thunder, rain, pressure and skyward violence.

Frost Giants thrive amid glaciers and frozen wastes. Flame Giants dwell among volcanic lands, burning mountains and molten depths. Cloud Giants claim high skies, floating citadels and rare atmospheric realms.

To smaller peoples, their scale makes them seem like landscapes with wills of their own.

THE STRUCTURE OF RACE IN AULON

Three great categories

BORN IN THE MATERIAL REALM

Mortal Races

Humans, dwarves, elves, sea dwellers, orcs, beastmen, naturefolk and giants. They are born, reproduce, build civilizations and die within material Aulon.

SHAPED BY COSMIC FORCE

Divine or Elemental Races

The Paragonium: elementals, Celestials, Luminas, Phantoms and other beings tied directly to planes, Spheres or ascended states.

REFINED AFTER DEATH

Ascended Races

The Zenithum and Nadirim, transformed from souls after death through the final refinements of Zenith or Nadir rather than biological descent.
