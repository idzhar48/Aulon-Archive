THE SUPREME ART OF THE LANCERS

When a Lancer becomes the hand of a Sphere

Among all abilities wielded by the Lancers of Aulon, none is more feared than RULE.

RULE is not merely an attack, spell, weapon technique or divine blessing. It is the temporary imposition of a Lancer’s will upon reality through complete resonance with their patron Sphere.

Only High Lancers and above may use RULE.

To invoke it, a Lancer must achieve full resonance with their Sphere, drawing that perfect connection outward into a single catastrophic manifestation. This resonance condenses into a primordial weapon shaped as a Lance—the symbolic and metaphysical expression of the Lancer’s authority.

When a Lancer uses RULE, they are not borrowing divine power in the ordinary sense.

They are momentarily acting as the hand of their Sphere.

THE MANIFESTATION OF THE LANCE

Divine authority given form

The Lance produced during RULE is not a physical weapon forged from metal, wood, crystal or ordinary magic.

It is divine authority given form.

It may appear differently depending on the Lancer and their patron Sphere, but its essence remains consistent: it is a concentrated vessel through which the Lancer declares a temporary law upon the world.

WILL ENCODING

Translating intention into divine law

The process by which a Lancer defines their RULE is known as Will Encoding.

A RULE may be vast enough to annihilate an empire in a single instant, or precise enough to pierce one target with a needle-thin beam moving at the speed of light. It may kill directly, erase matter, alter terrain, suspend forces, ignite landscapes, collapse space or impose lingering conditions upon the world.

  • A colossal beam of light
  • A storm of divine spears
  • A continent-spanning wave
  • A single invisible thrust
  • A spatial collapse
  • A field of death
  • A law of silence
  • A curse upon a region
  • A wound in gravity
  • A command embedded into the land itself

Theoretically, RULE is limitless in form.

Practically, its effects are limited by the Lancer’s rank, resonance, willpower, understanding and ability to define a coherent law.

A poorly conceived RULE may waste immense power.

A perfectly encoded RULE may end a war before anyone realizes it has begun.

THE TRUE SOUND OF THE SPHERE

The audible presence of divine law

When RULE is activated, the True Sound of the Lancer’s patron Sphere can be heard across enormous distances, sometimes nearly continents apart.

This sound is the Sphere’s Hymn. It is not merely noise. It is the audible presence of divine law.

Those who hear it often describe it as overwhelming, beautiful, terrifying or impossible to forget.

The primordial language does not function like ordinary mortal speech. Its words are vessels of intent, and their meaning expands or narrows depending on the resonance poured through them.

The same phrase may therefore become many different decrees.

THE RARITY OF RULE

Authority that cannot be used casually

Despite its overwhelming power, RULE is rarely used. A High Lancer reserves such authority for enemies, disasters and circumstances worthy of divine-level intervention.

  1. RULE is destructive by nature, even when precisely controlled.
  2. Its True Sound announces the Lancer’s involvement to gods, enemies, kingdoms, Emissaries and rival Lancers across vast distances.
  3. Misuse may damage the world the Lancer exists to protect.

Lancers who rely too easily on RULE are feared by allies as much as enemies.

A Lancer’s greatest power is also their greatest responsibility.

COUNTERING RULE

The difficulty of reversal

RULE can be stopped, but only under exceptional circumstances.

Because RULE is not ordinary magic, it cannot simply be dispelled. It is a law imposed upon reality. To counter it, one must either impose an opposing law of equal or greater authority, or contain its effect before it spreads beyond control.

Primary response

Counter-RULE

Impose an opposing law that perfectly reverses, interrupts or neutralizes the original effect.

Secondary response

Emissary Containment

Isolate the destructive law behind massive geometric barriers before it reaches the wider world.

Counter-RULE

The most direct way to negate RULE is through another RULE wielded by a Lancer of comparable power.

This is not simply a contest of strength.

It is a contest of law.

If the first RULE commands fire to erase a region, the second must do more than produce water or cold. It must impose a law that meaningfully prevents, redirects or invalidates the first. A poorly matched counter-RULE will fail. A perfectly defined counter-RULE can collapse the original effect entirely.

Each RULE isA decree
Each counter-RULE isAn appeal

Reality obeys the stronger and more coherent law.

EMISSARY CONTAINMENT

Geometry against catastrophe

The second method does not stop RULE directly. It isolates it.

Although Emissaries do not possess RULE and do not specialize in direct combat, they are masters of containment, mediation and divine structure. They can erect massive geometric barriers capable of isolating destructive forces from the wider world.

Lancers can also create barriers, but Emissaries do so with far greater precision and stability.

Three Emissaries may form a pyramid, but such a structure is usually insufficient against a true RULE attack. Four are generally required to seal a High Lancer’s RULE within a vast cubical barrier. Eight or more may be needed against an Arch Lancer’s RULE.

Against a Primarch Lancer’s RULE, the necessary containment is unknown.

It may require dozens of Emissaries.

It may require gods.

It may be impossible.

THE PURPOSE OF EMISSARIES

The stabilizers of divine power

Emissaries exist partly because Lancers are too dangerous to leave unchecked.

Aulon does not need only divine champions.

It needs divine stabilizers.

Emissaries protect the world by preventing conflicts from escalating into catastrophic displays of Spherebound force. They negotiate between divine interests, advise mortal powers, coordinate with religious institutions and contain metaphysical disasters when direct combat would cause even greater harm.

Their barriers are not merely defensive spells.

They are geometric declarations of containment.

They impose boundaries where Lancers impose force.
