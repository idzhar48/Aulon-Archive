CANON POWER SYSTEM · MORTAL HIERARCHY

The Adventurer Guild Ranking System of Aulon

A standardized measure of combat capability, survival experience and mission reliability—recognized by guilds, kingdoms, noble houses, military orders and private contractors across Aulon.

THE MORTAL LADDER OF POWER

Strength measured through metal

Unlike noble titles or military positions, an adventurer’s rank is fundamentally martial. Political influence, reputation and wealth often surround the higher ranks, but the system exists to classify what a mortal can reliably survive, confront and overcome.

Each rank represents roughly ten times the combat capability of the rank beneath it.

THE TENFOLD POWER RULE

Multiplicative dominance

Rank is not a simple progression. One Bronze is approximately equal to ten Iron fighters; one Silver to ten Bronze; one Gold to ten Silver. This pattern continues upward until an Adamantite carries the theoretical battle value of one hundred million Iron fighters or more.

Mithril battle value≈ 1,000,000 Iron
Orichalcum battle value≈ 10,000,000 Iron
Adamantite battle value≥ 100,000,000 Iron
Practical realityTactics still matter

Battle is never perfectly mathematical. Terrain, artifacts, intelligence, preparation and compatibility remain decisive. A Bronze may defeat several Iron fighters through discipline; a Gold may shatter a battlefield; an Adamantite may render armies irrelevant.

THE FINAL MORTAL RANK

Adamantite Adventurers

At Adamantite, a warrior ceases to be understood as merely heroic and begins to border on myth. They may withstand elemental catastrophes, duel divine-touched entities and alter the course of a war by appearing on a single battlefield.

  • Defeat elementals and ancient beasts.
  • Survive battles against ascendant entities.
  • Challenge weaker Lancers in direct combat.
  • Become known across the whole of Aulon.
  • Influence kings, guilds and empires through reputation alone.

Most kingdoms possess none. A powerful kingdom may possess one. An empire with several Adamantite figures is a terrifying global power.

Adamantites remain mortal. Their strength comes from training, talent, experience, artifacts, bloodlines, technique and willpower. They are the peak of mortal possibility, not divine by nature.

THE DIVINE THRESHOLD

Adamantite Adventurers and Lancers

Lancers are not simply stronger adventurers. They are mortals tethered directly to the Spheres. Sphere Resonance makes their strength divine, symbolic and metaphysically reinforced.

An Adamantite may plausibly defeat a newly awakened, inexperienced, overconfident or poorly matched Initiate Lancer. A Veteran Lancer presents a far greater challenge. A High Lancer usually wins. An Arch Lancer overwhelmingly outclasses the mortal.

Exceptions remain possible through divine artifacts, superior tactics, battlefield preparation, intimate knowledge of a weakness, timing and sacrifice.
