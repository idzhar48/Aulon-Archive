Lancers, Emissaries, and Blighted Lancers

The three Spherebound orders whose bodies, souls and resonance have been permanently altered by powers greater than mortal life was ever meant to bear.

THE SPHEREBOUND HIERARCHIES

Beyond the mortal ladder

Beyond the mortal ranking systems of guilds and kingdoms exists a far older and more terrifying hierarchy: the order of the Spherebound.

These are beings whose existence has been altered by direct contact with divine resonance. Some become warriors. Some become diplomats. Some become instruments of corruption. All are changed permanently by forces greater than mortal life was ever meant to bear.

Divine champions

Lancers

Mortals tethered to the Spheres as direct warriors within the material realm.

Though similar in origin, their purposes, abilities and relationships to the Spheres differ dramatically.

THE LANCERS

Champions of the Spheres

Lancers are mortals chosen and tethered by the Spheres to act as their direct champions within the material realm.

Unlike adventurers, whose power derives from training, discipline, artifacts, bloodline, experience and personal mastery, Lancers draw strength from Sphere Resonance. Their souls are bound to a patron Sphere, allowing them to channel divine principles through a mortal vessel.

This makes Lancers fundamentally different from even the greatest adventurers.

A mortal may become Adamantite through effort.
A Lancer becomes something more than mortal through tethering.

The transformation is not merely symbolic. Upon becoming a Lancer, the host’s body, soul and resonance are altered to withstand divine power. They become longer-lived, harder to kill and capable of feats that violate ordinary limitations.

Their rank is determined not by guild authority, but by the depth of their resonance with their patron Sphere.

Initiate Lancer

An Initiate Lancer is newly tethered or only shallowly harmonized with their patron Sphere.

Though already more powerful than nearly all ordinary mortals, Initiates may still be vulnerable to Adamantite adventurers, powerful artifacts or experienced monster-slaying orders.

Their power is immense, but not yet refined.

Veteran Lancer

A Veteran Lancer has survived long enough to understand the burden of divine resonance.

At this rank, the Lancer’s abilities become more stable, their body more thoroughly adapted and their command over Sphere-aligned powers significantly more dangerous.

Most mortals will never encounter a Veteran Lancer in their lifetime.

High Lancer

A High Lancer is a fully realized divine champion.

At this level, the Lancer gains access to one of the most terrifying abilities in existence:

ULTIMATE SPHEREBOUND AUTHORITY

RULE — Rho Und Lar Ekhala

RULE warps reality through the authority of the patron Sphere. It may manifest as devastating instant-kill phenomena, region-level destruction or precise reality alteration depending on the Lancer, Sphere and will involved.

Only High Lancers and above may wield RULE. It does not merely attack. It commands reality to obey.

Arch Lancer

An Arch Lancer is a near-mythical divine vessel whose resonance approaches the limits of mortal containment.

Arch Lancers can devastate armies, challenge ascendant beings, confront catastrophic entities and impose the will of their Sphere across vast regions. Even Adamantite adventurers are usually outclassed before them.

To fight an Arch Lancer is to fight the shadow of a god.

Primarch Lancer

The Primarch Lancer is an exceptional and nearly singular category reserved for beings whose tether approaches cosmic significance.

A Primarch is not merely a stronger Lancer.

They are a turning point in history.

The known or prophesied example of this rank is Aton’s Singular Chosen, the Avatar of Aton, who does not emerge until the later ages. This figure is expected to reveal truths concerning Aton’s location, nature and role within the cosmos, potentially altering the structure of reality itself.

In later ages, the title also gains terrible significance through Ena’s Apostle—a Blighted Primarch Lancer of Disruption destined to oppose the Avatar of Aton.

EMISSARIES

The non-combat Spherebound

Not every Spherebound being is created for war.

Some Spheres prefer influence, negotiation, guidance, prophecy, knowledge, strategy, law, beauty, craft or emotional manipulation over direct violence. For these purposes, they create Emissaries.

Emissaries are spherebound immortals formed through processes similar to Lancers, but unlike Lancers, they do not wield outright combat authority. They do not exist primarily as battlefield champions and do not possess access to RULE.

Their influence is subtler.

  • Diplomacy
  • Counsel
  • Divine intervention
  • Strategy
  • Ritual authority
  • Negotiation
  • Prophecy
  • Cultural guidance
  • Political manipulation
  • Theological interpretation
Where Lancers areBlades
Emissaries areVoices

Where Lancers conquer battlefields, Emissaries alter the conditions that make battles inevitable.

Emissary ranks

There are two principal ranks: Emissary and High Emissary.

A standard Emissary acts as the representative and instrument of a Sphere’s will within mortal affairs.

A High Emissary possesses deeper authority, greater resonance and broader divine jurisdiction. They may influence kingdoms, alter religious movements, guide divine contracts or speak with power approaching that of high celestial officials.

Although they are not battlefield entities in the same sense as Lancers, only fools mistake Emissaries for harmless beings.

Words may move more kingdoms than swords.

BLIGHTED LANCERS

The corrupted Spherebound

The beings tethered to Ena and the Seven Eldritch Sins are not true Lancers in the same sacred sense as those bound to the Spheres of Aulon.

They are incomplete imitations.

Ena and the Palinoian Sins have not mastered the soul. They do not fully understand Weight, Tune, resonance or the delicate stability that allows a soul to persist without collapsing under divine pressure.

As a result, those bound to them cannot truly harmonize with their eldritch overlords. Instead, their souls are corrupted beyond salvation. Their bodies become husks. Their will is eroded. Their actions are manipulated by the incomprehensible desires of the Outer Sins.

Within the Veil, the soul of a Blighted Lancer does not immediately disintegrate as it would beyond reality. Instead, it becomes twisted, stained and hollowed while still bound to the body. This creates an abomination that can operate inside Aulon while carrying the resonance of the Outside.

They are among the most dangerous agents of Ena.

Not because they are free.

But because they are not.

Blighted Lancer ranks

For ease of comparison, Blighted Lancers use the same general rank structure as true Lancers:

Blighted InitiateBlighted VeteranBlighted HighBlighted ArchBlighted Primarch

These ranks do not reflect healthy resonance. They reflect the degree of corruption, compatibility and destructive output forced through the host.

The higher the rank, the less of the original person remains.

THE APOSTLE OF ENA

The Blighted Primarch Lancer of Disruption

Above all other Blighted Lancers stands the prophesied and catastrophic figure known as The Apostle of Ena.

This being is the Blighted Primarch Lancer of Disruption, tethered directly to Ena herself rather than to one of her Seven Sins.

Where ordinary Lancers channel a Sphere’s divine principle, the Apostle channels Ena’s discordant essence—corruption, disruption, blight and anti-harmonic existence.

The Apostle is not simply a servant.

He is the closest thing Ena has to a true champion within the Veil.

His existence represents the possibility that Ena may one day succeed in doing what she has failed to do since the Null Age: bind a soul strongly enough to carry her will without instantly destroying it.

For this reason, the Apostle is more than an enemy.

He is a breakthrough.

A proof of concept.

A living wound in the Symphony.

In the later ages, he is destined to stand against Aton’s Singular Chosen—the Avatar of Aton, the Primarch Lancer of the Creator himself.

ATON’S SINGULAR CHOSEN

The Avatar of Aton

Unlike the Spheres, Aton does not ordinarily appoint champions.

He predates godhood, hierarchy, worship and cosmic governance. He is the First Conductor, the origin from which all Ephemeromorphs emerged.

Yet in the later ages, one being is said to arise as Aton’s Singular Chosen: The Avatar of Aton.

This figure is the Primarch Lancer of the Creator.

He does not belong fully to the existing Spherebound order, because his patron is not merely one Sphere among many. Aton is the infinite medium in which all Spheres, realms and laws exist.

The Avatar’s emergence in the Fifth or Sixth Age marks a turning point in cosmic history. Through him, the truth of Aton’s location, nature and continued presence may finally be revealed to the world.

He may also change the way reality itself functions on a cosmic level.

If the Spheres are instruments, and the Lancers are those who wield their echoes, then the Avatar of Aton is the hand of the Conductor returning to the Symphony.
