THE LAWS OF RESONANCE

The principles reality obeys

Beneath the visible workings of magic, divinity, souls, Spheres and the Veil lie the foundational principles known as the Twelve Cosmic Laws of Aulon.

Scholars, theologians and Spherebound philosophers call them the Laws of Resonance, for they describe how beings, realms, souls and divine powers interact within the Symphony of creation.

These Laws are believed to originate from Aton himself, whether as intentional design, inevitable consequence or the underlying structure of his infinite being.

They are not laws written in scripture.

They are laws reality obeys.

Ⅰ
First Cosmic Law

The Law of Creation

All existence originates from Aton, the First Conductor of the Cosmic Symphony.

Matter, energy, souls, Spheres, planes, magic and all cosmic principles are expressions of the first act of creation. Nothing within known existence exists independently from Aton’s primordial will.

Even corruption has its origin in creation.

Even Ena, despite her exile and discord, was born from Aton.

This Law establishes the deepest truth of the cosmos:

Nothing is truly outside Aton’s origin.

The Outside may stand beyond the Veil, but even Ena began within the first Symphony.

Ⅱ
Second Cosmic Law

The Law of the Sphere

Reality is structured through Spheres.

Each Sphere governs a fundamental domain of existence, such as Light, Life, Death, Fire, Space, Magic, Time, Nature, War, Knowledge or Justice.

The Spheres are not merely powerful beings who control these forces.

They are the living principles through which these forces retain meaning.

FireBurns through its Sphere
TimeMoves through its Sphere
MagicFunctions through Arcane Law
RealityRetains structure

Every god, Lancer, elemental, divine artifact and metaphysical entity draws power from either one Sphere or a resonance between multiple Spheres.

Without the Spheres, reality would lose structure.

Ⅲ
Third Cosmic Law

The Law of Resonance

A being may wield the power of a Sphere only if their soul resonates with it.

Resonance depends primarily on two spiritual qualities: Weight and Tune.

Weight refers to the strength, magnitude and density of the soul.

Tune refers to the soul’s alignment with the nature of a Sphere or cosmic force.

A mortal whose Tune aligns with Fire may be more suited to Kyntus. A soul aligned with judgment may resonate with Aebu. One aligned with death, darkness and finality may draw the attention of Omongo.

This Law determines whether a being can become Spherebound, Emissary, Lancer, Arch Lancer or Primarch Lancer.

Power is not granted randomly.

The soul must be able to sing with the god.

Ⅳ
Fourth Cosmic Law

The Law of the Soul

Every soul possesses three essential aspects:

WeightSpiritual magnitude, depth, devotion and power.
TuneHarmonic alignment with cosmic forces and other souls.
EchoMemory, imprint or residue of past incarnations and experiences.

Weight determines the potential force a soul may carry.

Tune determines where that soul belongs in the cosmic Symphony.

Echo determines what remains of the soul’s previous movements through existence.

A soul with great Weight but unstable Tune may become dangerous. A soul with beautiful Tune but little Weight may be spiritually pure yet metaphysically weak. A soul with a powerful Echo may carry memories, instincts, talents, traumas or unfinished resonance from prior existence.

Together, these three aspects determine a soul’s destiny, afterlife and capacity for ascension.

Ⅴ
Fifth Cosmic Law

The Law of Ascension

Mortals may ascend through resonance.

Ascension is not limited to one path. Across the ages, beings have risen from ordinary mortality into higher states through devotion, power, transformation, divine selection or post-mortal refinement.

Adventurer → SphereboundSpherebound → LancerLancer → Arch LancerSoul → AngelSoul → DaemonSoul → Elemental or Celestial

True ascension requires harmony between Weight and Tune.

Without sufficient Weight, the soul cannot withstand higher resonance.

Without proper Tune, the soul cannot align with the force transforming it.

This is why ascension is rare.

The soul must be strong enough to endure change and aligned enough not to shatter beneath it.

Ⅵ
Sixth Cosmic Law

The Law of RULE

Only High Lancers and above may wield RULE.

RULE is the supreme expression of Spherebound resonance, allowing a Lancer to temporarily impose a divine law upon reality through the manifestation of a Grade 9 Primordial Lance.

  • Rewrite local physical laws
  • Cause instantaneous annihilation
  • Collapse spatial regions
  • Alter probability
  • Reshape environments
  • Impose Sphere-aligned conditions

However, RULE cannot freely override the Laws of the Soul.

A soul may be damaged, displaced, corrupted, judged, sealed or severed under specific conditions, but the fundamental metaphysics of Weight, Tune and Echo cannot be casually erased by ordinary RULE.

This limitation is crucial.

Even divine authority has boundaries.

Ⅶ
Seventh Cosmic Law

The Law of Balance

No Sphere may dominate the cosmos completely.

Opposing principles always exist to preserve equilibrium.

Light↔Void
Life↔Death
Creation↔Corruption
Order↔Chaos
Growth↔Decay
Freedom↔Fate
Memory↔Forgetting

Balance does not mean peace.

It does not mean justice, comfort or happiness.

It means no force may become absolute.

Aulon may suffer war, disaster, plague and collapse while still remaining cosmically balanced. Conversely, a world of endless peace and unchecked growth may become dangerously unstable if no opposing principle restrains it.

The Law of Balance is why the Spheres tolerate suffering.

Equilibrium is not kindness. It is survival.

Ⅷ
Eighth Cosmic Law

The Law of the Veil

The Veil separates the harmonic realms of Aulon from the Outside.

Within the Veil, souls remain stable, reality obeys Sphere law, matter and magic coexist, and time and space maintain coherent interaction.

Beyond the Veil, these protections fail.

Souls collapse under non-harmonic pressure. Reality becomes unstable. Cosmic forces cease to behave according to the Laws of the Spheres.

Only certain beings can survive outside the Veil, and even fewer can do so without being altered, claimed or destroyed.

The Veil is therefore not simply a barrier.

It is the condition that allows Aulon to exist as Aulon.

Ⅸ
Ninth Cosmic Law

The Law of Corruption

When a soul resonates with incomplete, false or incompatible divinity, corruption occurs.

This is the origin of Blighted Lancers.

Corruption is not merely moral failure. It is a metaphysical distortion of the soul’s Tune and Weight caused by exposure to forces that cannot harmonize properly with Aulon’s Symphony.

  • Loss of free will
  • Warped resonance
  • Physical mutation
  • Spiritual instability
  • Emotional distortion
  • Eventual soul degradation

This phenomenon originates most directly from Ena and the Palinoian Sins.

Where true resonance elevates, corruption hollows.

Ⅹ
Tenth Cosmic Law

The Law of the Blight

Souls bound to Ena or the Seven Sins cannot achieve true resonance.

Instead of harmonizing with their patron, they are overtaken by dissonance.

  • Blighted Lancers
  • Apostles of corruption
  • Vessels of discord
  • Husks carrying damaged souls
  • Instruments of the Outside within the Veil

Their power grows not through harmony, but through decay.

A true Lancer becomes stronger by aligning more perfectly with their Sphere. A Blighted Lancer becomes stronger as their soul becomes less their own.

The Law of the Blight reveals Ena’s greatest limitation:

She can grant power.

But she cannot yet preserve the soul that carries it.

Ⅺ
Eleventh Cosmic Law

The Law of Singular Champions

Each Sphere can manifest only a limited number of high-order champions at any given time.

Arch LancersRare
Primarch LancersRarer still
One Primarch-level figureUsually per cosmic era

This Law preserves the balance of power across Aulon. If every Sphere could endlessly produce Arch Lancers or Primarchs, divine conflict would devastate creation beyond repair.

The scarcity of Singular Champions prevents the world from collapsing under the weight of its own gods.

A Primarch is therefore not merely a warrior.

A Primarch is an event.

Their emergence indicates that the age itself has become unstable enough to require cosmic intervention.

Ⅻ
Twelfth Cosmic Law

The Law of Revelation

Aton does not intervene directly in the cosmos under ordinary circumstances.

Though all existence originates from him, he remains distant, expanded and seemingly absent from the affairs of Spheres, mortals, Lancers and realms.

When Aton’s will becomes known, it does not usually arrive through open command.

It appears through revelation.

AvatarsPrimarch LancersWorld-changing eventsLost HymnsCosmic disastersRuptures in the VeilTruths uncovered at the end of an age

In the distant ages, the Avatar of Aton is expected to reveal truths concerning the origin of the universe, the nature of the Veil and the true place of Aton within existence.

This Law is the final and most mysterious of the Twelve.

For it suggests that Aton’s silence is not absence.

It is waiting.
