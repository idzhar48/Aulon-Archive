EKHARA’S SHADOW

The High Forbidden Words

Among the surviving Forbidden Lexicon are structured invocations far more dangerous than ordinary corrupted phrases. Scholars of blight, heresy and ancient language call them the High Forbidden Words—whispers placed by Ena and her Palinoians into mortal dreams, rituals and desperate mouths.

Lesser words twist flesh, summon minions or erode the soul. High Words possess ritual coherence. They do not merely damage the speaker.

They answer them.

These words form the foundation of Moonless Magic, the creation of Marked Ones and the earliest transformation toward Blighted Lancerhood.

They are not true Ekhara. They are Ekhara’s shadow.

EIN
EXALT THE HALLOWED ONE

Ein-Torra

MEANINGExalt the Hallowed One
EFFECTThe Witnessing of the Dark Cosmos

When spoken with sufficient Will, the sky itself becomes a watching thing. Countless eyes of the dark cosmos bloom across the heavens like wounds in the firmament. They do not descend, speak or attack. They simply observe the user as though answering an ancient cry:

Witness me.

This is not a conventional summoning, but a recognition event. Something beyond the Veil acknowledges the speaker, and the attention of the dark cosmos falls briefly upon the world.

Those who meet the eyes directly experience more than fear: the realization of being perceived by something too vast, ancient and incompatible with mortal existence. Victims may become paralyzed, unable to move, speak, cast or defend themselves. Strong warriors falter. Monsters freeze.

The eyes do not attack.

They witness. And to be witnessed by them is enough.

BRALL
ORISON OF THE BROKEN

Brall-Durr’

MEANINGOrison of the Broken
EFFECTSoul Echolocation

Brall-Durr’ is a hunting word. It reveals nearby souls as distorted pulses, ripples and echoes moving through unseen layers of reality.

The weaker a soul’s Weight and Tune, the clearer its ripple becomes. Spiritual trauma, unstable Tune, weak Will and poor resonance illuminate the people easiest to corrupt.

◌Strong Weight
◎Unstable Tune
◉Spiritual Trauma
●Vulnerable Prey

The word does not corrupt. It searches. It listens for fracture. It reveals where the soul is easiest to break.

Cults of Ena and the Palinoians use it before recruitment, sacrifice, possession and the preparation of vessels. It belongs not to conquerors, but predators.

ZOR
SERVITUDE ETERNAL TO EXODUS OF THE COSMOS

Zor Eth Xalakt

MEANINGServitude Eternal to Exodus of the Cosmos
EFFECTBinding Vow and Sigil Branding

Of all known High Forbidden Words, Zor Eth Xalakt is among the most consequential. It is not merely an invocation.

It is a vow.

Spoken with Will, it pledges the user to Ena or a Palinoian god. The chosen patron responds by branding the speaker with a unique Sigil—not drawn on the body, but forced into it.

The mark burns through skin, soul, memory and resonance, restructuring the mortal into a partial instrument of the patron. Many collapse immediately, their souls forfeited to Ena and the Palinoians. Only sufficient Will, devotion, obsession, desperation or hatred allows survival.

Those who endure become Marked Ones.

MORTALS BRANDED BY THE OUTSIDE

The Marked Ones

Marked Ones are not Blighted Lancers—not yet. They are mortals who survived Sigil Branding and gained one or two forms of Moonless Magic without completing Blighted metamorphosis.

APPEARANCE

Still mortal

They may retain humanity, sanity, personality, memory, love and fear while hiding their marks beneath clothing, armor or illusion.

POWER

Moonless Magic

They carry intrusive powers from beyond the Veil while moving through guilds, cities and ordinary society.

DESTINY

Slow corruption

The Sigil gradually erodes identity, deepens obedience and reshapes thought until its bearer becomes an instrument of chaos.

THE VEIL ANOMALY

Power that severs possibility

Branding cuts the Marked One away from the lawful Symphony. They cannot hear or witness true Godly Hymns, receive ordinary Spherical Boons, or form contracts with non-Palinoian gods.

Their spiritual exile also removes them from ordinary Soul Resonance. Lawful Spheres and their Lancers cannot detect a Marked One—or even a Blighted Lancer—by resonance alone despite their physical presence in Aulon.

They are revealed only by what remains observable: the Moonless spells they cast, the Sigils branded upon their bodies and the eerie glow of their eyes when the Veil thins.

Ena’s servants walk beneath the gods’ hearing.

The tragedy lies in delay. A Marked One is not immediately a monster. They may continue as adventurers, mercenaries, wanderers, cultists, scholars or citizens. Yet dependence grows, the patron’s resonance deepens, and the self recedes.

POWER WITHOUT AULLA’S LIGHT

Moonless Magic

Moonless Magic flows through Palinoian Sigils. It depends neither on the Bright Moon’s light, Aulla’s reflection nor the lawful resonance of the Seven Moons. It draws instead from Ena’s Reservoir and Palinoian patronage.

Ordinary magic harmonizes with Aulon. Moonless Magic intrudes, forcing effects into reality from beyond the world’s proper structure.

  • Cleaves what should not be cut
  • Binds the incorporeal
  • Replicates bodies without souls
  • Freezes unnatural stasis
  • Removes meaningful interaction
  • Creates impossible corrosive matter

Its power is immense.

Its cost is spiritual exile.

ETHEREAL CHAIN MAGIC

Sigil of the Chained

Produces chains capable of binding mortal and spiritual beings. They may restrain bodies, trap souls, suppress movement, interrupt magic or tether entities across planes according to the user’s strength.

Unlike ordinary binding spells, these chains affect beings without stable physical forms: ghosts, apparitions, spirits, daemons and even certain divine-touched entities. The Sigil embodies control, imprisonment, domination and forced obedience.

⋈
ELDRITCH CUTTING AND BITING MAGIC

Sigil of the Jaw

Manifests invisible bites, tearing arcs, serrated void-lines and monstrous cutting forces that seem to chew through reality. It can cleave nearly anything within Aulon, terrifying armored warriors, barrier mages and creatures dependent on physical durability.

Where a blade cuts from one edge to another, the Jaw bites directly into the concept of wholeness.

◇
REPLICATION · SPECTRAL DOLL MAGIC

Sigil of the Mirror

Creates perfect replicas of living beings, including the user. They imitate appearance, voice, movement and mannerisms flawlessly, but possess no souls—form without spiritual essence.

Spectral Doll Magic lets the bearer manipulate replicas, shells and puppet-like constructs. This makes the Sigil a weapon of espionage, assassination, infiltration, psychological warfare and forbidden experiments in identity.

It does not create life. It creates likeness.

□
PERFECT STASIS · PHANTASMAL ISOLATION

Sigil of the Hermit

Freezes the user or another being in an unchanging state, suspending motion, decay, harm, transformation or progression. It may preserve, defend, imprison or isolate a target from the world’s natural flow.

Phantasmal Isolation separates its victim from meaningful contact with reality, trapping them in stillness, solitude, illusion or suspended awareness.

The Hermit is not merely preservation. It is removal. Sometimes to be frozen is to be forgotten.

∞
ZIRION · DIRECT CONDUIT TO ENA

Sigil of the Perfect Host

An extraordinary and possibly unique mark belonging to Zirion. Unlike ordinary Sigils, the Perfect Host connects directly to Ena, siphoning unlimited Eldritch Mana from the Cradle of Sin—Ena’s Basin—and maintaining constant contact with the Blighted Goddess.

  • Unlimited Eldritch Mana
  • Constant contact with Ena
  • Copy other Marked skills
  • Enhance other Marked Ones
  • Enhanced strength and durability
  • Psychic invulnerability
  • Mind acceleration ×100
  • Resistance to memory rupture

The direct link to Ena’s incomprehensible consciousness protects Zirion from psychic mind-tearing attacks, including overwhelming illusion, mental imprisonment and forced memory rupture sometimes used by desperate Emissaries.

This is more than a powerful mark. It is a prototype of possession without collapse—Ena’s attempt to make a vessel capable of holding her influence without immediately disintegrating.

DERIVATIVE ANOMALY · CORROSIVE SPIKES

Sigil of the BogBeast

The BogBeast emerged from Zirion, soul-splitting, artificial replication and Palinoian binding through a chain of forbidden violations.

01 · MIRRORA replica of Zirion is created
02 · SOULZirion divides and imbues part of his soul
03 · HERMITThe replica is bound into permanent stasis
04 · ANOMALYThe BogBeast condition emerges

Soul-splitting was learned by Zirion and is not an inherent function of the Perfect Host. Under these compounded violations, the Sigil mutated.

The BogBeast produces innumerable blade-sharp spikes from the body. They may be detached as swords, launched as projectiles or grown in overwhelming numbers for assault and defense. Their acid melts any substance in Aulon except Grade 9 items.

Its existence proves that marks can be copied, souls divided, vessels frozen—and new Sigils born from the collision of those violations.

PAIN AS THE FIRST TRIAL

The Branding Process

Every Sigil is born through extreme agony that is physical, spiritual, metaphysical and existential.

ⅠThe body burns
ⅡThe soul twists
ⅢThe Tune cracks
ⅣThe Weight is dragged Outside

The patron cannot truly harmonize with the mortal soul. Survival requires unyielding Will and undying devotion. Hesitation, regret, resistance or failure forfeits the soul to Ena and the Palinoians.

Cults call survival proof of worthiness.

In truth, it proves only that the soul has not broken yet.

THE PRICE OF THE MARK

Separation, suspicion and decay

After Branding, lawful Hymns become muffled or silent. The Marked One can no longer receive non-Palinoian boons or sacred contracts. They still walk among mortals, but no longer fully belong to Aulon’s Symphony.

At first they may believe power came without loss. Many hide among adventurers and conceal their marks from companions and guilds. Yet past murders by corrupted Marked Ones have made suspicion justified.

Every hidden bearer lives beneath three fears: discovery, decay and what they are becoming.

STABILIZING THE MARK

Ascension into Blighted Lancerhood

The only known way to survive a Sigil’s long-term strain is metamorphosis into a Blighted Lancer—another trial often worse than Branding.

Body and soul restructure to withstand Eldritch strain. The Sigil stabilizes, corrupted resonance deepens and the bearer can carry greater Palinoian power without immediate collapse.

This is not salvation. It is adaptation. The Marked One survives by becoming less human.

Those who endure the greatest strain before metamorphosis may emerge as the strongest Blighted Lancers, creating a cruel cultic incentive:

Suffering becomes cultivation.
Damage becomes potential.
Corruption becomes preparation.

THE THIRD PATH TO POWER

Three roads through Aulon’s hierarchy

THE FIRST PATH

Harmony

The Spheres, Ekhara, Emissaries and Lancers. Power through lawful resonance and divine blessing.

THE SECOND PATH

Mastery

Adventurers, discipline, artifacts and mortal excellence. Power earned through training and will.

THE THIRD PATH

Corruption

Forbidden Words, Sigils, Moonless Magic, Marked Ones and Blighted Lancers. Power purchased through spiritual exile.

Corruption seduces because it asks for neither a Sphere’s blessing, guild advancement, decades of training nor natural resonance. The Marked One gains power quickly—then spends the rest of life discovering what was taken in exchange.
