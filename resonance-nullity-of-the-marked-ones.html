THE VEIL ANOMALY OF THE MARKED ONES

Physically present. Spiritually absent.

Among the most terrifying consequences of Palinoian Branding is not the power it grants, but the silence it creates.

When a mortal becomes a Marked One through forbidden covenant—especially through the High Forbidden Word Zor Eth Xalakt—they are branded by Ena or one of the Palinoians and permanently severed from the lawful resonance of the Spheres. This severance is known as Resonance Nullity.

Marked Ones still breathe, move, speak, bleed and act within the Material Realm. They may walk through cities, join guilds, fight as adventurers, serve as spies or hide among ordinary people. Yet to gods, Lancers and Emissaries who perceive the world through Soul Resonance, they are silent.

Ⅰ
SEVERANCE FROM SPHERICAL BOONS

The soul unregistered

All ordinary beings in Aulon exist within the Resonance Web of the Spheres. Even unbelievers possess souls born from Aulla’s Light, carrying Weight, Tune and Echo. Their existence produces a faint spiritual resonance that divine beings may perceive.

Branding cuts that connection. A Marked One can no longer receive lawful blessings, form sacred contracts with non-Palinoian Spheres or tether as a true Lancer. Their Tune is corrupted beyond lawful recognition; their soul becomes unregistered within the Song of Aulon.

THE CLOSED PATH

No lawful ascension

Once a person becomes Marked, the path of lawful divine ascension is closed. They may still gain power—but never again through harmony.

THE INTACT BODY

Not physical invisibility

They remain visible to ordinary eyes, audible to ears and vulnerable to blades. They leave footprints, bleed, cast shadows and can be followed by mundane investigation.

What fails is divine perception. Gods, Lancers, Emissaries, Hymnic awareness, Soul Resonance and ordinary Spherical Boons cannot directly detect them.

ENA’S INVISIBLE ARMY

Outside the Symphony’s hearing

Resonance Nullity gives Ena one of her greatest strategic advantages within Aulon. Marked Ones, cultists, corrupted adventurers, Sigil-bearers and Blighted Lancers can operate beyond the awareness of her siblings, avoiding the divine senses that normally warn the world of spiritual threats.

A Marked One may infiltrate a guild.
A Blighted Lancer may approach a city unnoticed.
A cult of Ena may form beneath a temple.
An Emissary may miss a Palinoian servant in the same chamber.
A Lancer may be ambushed by an enemy they could not hear coming.
A hidden Sigil-bearer may live among ordinary people for years.

Ena’s forces do not need to overpower the Spheres in every conflict. Sometimes they only need to remain unnoticed.

They are not stronger than the whole Symphony. They are outside its hearing.

Ⅲ
DETECTION OF THE MARKED

Three principal signs

Because Soul Resonance fails, the Marked must be identified through indirect evidence.

1MOONLESS MAGIC

The spell that does not sing

Moonless Magic fails to reflect Aulla’s Light or harmonize with Aeynu’s Arcane Law. Experienced observers recognize unnatural flow, absent harmony, Eldritch residue, Blight-like distortion and effects that function without lunar reflection.

  • Unnatural spell flow
  • Absence of harmonic resonance
  • Eldritch residue
  • Violations of ordinary Arcane Law

It does not sing. It intrudes.

2THE SIGIL

The mark of ownership

Every Marked One bears a Sigil unique to body, soul and patron. It may resemble a tattoo, brand, scar, wound, burn or shifting Eldritch pattern, concealed by clothing, armor, illusion or deliberate mutilation.

Once revealed, the truth is difficult to deny. The mark is not decoration. It is ownership.

3THE ECLIPSE EYES

The Outside shining through

When the Veil thins—especially during the Cyclical Eclipse—their eyes may glow with Eldritch light, reflect the dark cosmos or take on the pattern and emotional resonance of a patron Palinoian.

During ordinary days, the Marked may hide among mortals. During the Eclipse, the body remembers what owns it.

WHY THE SPHERES CANNOT HEAR THEM

A failure of method, not might

The Spheres understand reality through Tune, Weight, harmony, Hymn and divine relationship. All lawful existence within Aulon produces spiritual sound. Ena’s corruption dampens Tune and fractures resonance instead.

The gods are not blind because they are weak. They are blind because their perception depends upon a kind of music the Marked no longer possess.

To the Spheres, a Marked One is not a loud enemy hiding in shadow. They are a silence shaped like a person.

THE AURIC CHOIR OF THE VEIL · HIGH OSTRA

The Unheard Inquisition

In response to Resonance Nullity, High Ostra established a secretive order dedicated to hunting, studying and containing the Marked. Formally the Auric Choir of the Veil, it is known among commoners, hidden cults and some Lancers as the Unheard Choir.

“If they cannot be heard, then we must learn to listen differently.”

Purpose of the Order

  • Hunt Marked Ones
  • Investigate Moonless Magic
  • Study Forbidden Words
  • Preserve fragments of Ekhara
  • Develop Nullity detection
  • Assist Lancers and Emissaries
  • Protect High Ostra from infiltration
  • Prevent Blighted Lancer emergence

The Order operates secretly because public knowledge that the gods cannot always hear their enemies could fracture faith itself.

STRUCTURE OF THE AURIC CHOIR

Those trained to notice silence

ⅠFIELD OBSERVERS

Listeners

Study observation, magical theory, basic detection, interrogation and investigation. Their task is to notice silence.

ⅡMID-TIER OPERATIVES

Resonants

Identify distortion in light, resonance, magic and behavior. They ask what should be present—but is not.

ⅢELITE HUNTERS

Arbiters

Authorized to confront confirmed Marked Ones using anti-Sigil combat, Lightbound Chains and containment.

ⅣHIGH SCHOLARS

Cantors

Study Ekhara, Hymns, Forbidden Words and corrupted Sigils. Many go mad. Some hear whispers back.

ⅤHIDDEN LEADERSHIP

Grand Choir

Sage Prima magi, theologians and perhaps an Emissary decide what is investigated, contained, erased or hidden.

An Arbiter’s duty is grim. They do not merely hunt monsters. They hunt people who may still remember being human.

SIGNATURE METHODS OF THE ORDER

Listening differently

SYNCHRONIZED DETECTION

Chorus Formation

Members combine spells, voices and instruments to amplify weak signs of Nullity and triangulate silent presences, drawing upon ancient Atonite choir principles.

LAYERED DETECTION FIELDS

Silent Net

Forces Marked Ones to reveal themselves through movement, absence, light distortion or barrier interaction during raids, lockdowns and cult investigations.

INVESTIGATIVE RITE

Judicium Nullis

Identifies absent resonance and corrupted magical use. It cannot prove guilt alone, but determines whether deeper examination is required.

DISTRICT CONTAINMENT

Veil Lock Protocol

Seals a district, building, ruin or battlefield with layered barriers, suppressing Moonless activity and allowing Arbiters to sweep the area.

WEAPONS OF THE ORDER

Instruments against the unheard

CONTROLLED DIVINE FREQUENCY

Hymn-Resonance Blades

Not Skau’Run and without Grade 9 authority, but effective at disrupting low- to mid-level Marked Ones.

ANTI-SIGIL RESTRAINT

Lightbound Chains

Reinforced bindings restrain the Marked physically and spiritually while preventing Sigil activation.

ARCANE LIGHT FILTER

Null Lanterns

Reveal distortion zones. A Marked One may appear within the glow as an absence, stain, silhouette or warped outline.

THE ORDER’S LIMITS

Danger in the diagnosis

FALSE POSITIVES

Silence does not always mean guilt

Trauma, curses, rare magical conditions and divine interference can mimic Resonance Nullity. The Order has likely condemned innocents before.

HIGH-LEVEL SIGILS

The hunted learn to hide

Advanced Marked Ones can mask distortion, forge resonance patterns and manipulate detection fields. Against Blighted Lancers, the Choir can rarely act alone.
