THE BEQUEATHED WEAPONS OF THE SPHERES

Divine function given form

A Skau’Run is the personal divine weapon of a Lancer or Emissary—a soul-bound Grade 9 construct granted through direct resonance with a Sphere. Its name derives from Schar, God of War, Honor and Sacrifice, whose domain governs weapons, martial expression and the sacred meaning of combat.

It is not forged in a mortal smithy or made from ordinary metal, wood, bone, crystal or stone. When summoned, it becomes the physical shape through which bearer and patron express their relationship.

NATURE OF THE SKAU’RUN

A divine relationship, not merely a weapon

All Skau’Run are Grade 9 Primordial constructs, sharing the tier of the Lance manifested during RULE while remaining stable personal weapons rather than single-use reality decrees.

AUTHORIZED BEARERS

Spherebound only

Lancers, Emissaries, certain dual-mode Spherebound and perhaps rare Primarch-level beings may wield them safely.

MORTAL DANGER

The unworthy hand

Touching one without permission or compatibility may damage the body, rupture the soul or force it back into the cycle.

SOUL-BINDING

Held beyond sight

Once summoned, the weapon binds until dismissed, sealed or the bearer dies, remaining latent within the spirit when not visible.

An Arch Lancer may call their weapon as naturally as breathing. A wounded Veteran may need stance, chant and concentration. A failing Initiate may summon only sparks of its form.

∅
FAILURE TO WIELD

Invocation is only the first trial

A weakened Lancer may summon their Skau’Run and still fail to wield it. The construct may appear incomplete, become too heavy to lift, burn the hand, reject command or send feedback through the bearer’s soul.

Weakened resonanceUnstable Soul WeightDisrupted TuneDamaged bodyCompromised WillWithheld patron strengthCorrupted tether

A Skau’Run does not obey rank alone. It obeys resonance.

ARCHETYPE PERMANENCE

The form changes. The function remains.

Every Sphere possesses a fixed Skau’Run archetype. Ornamentation, size, apparent material, color and cultural expression may differ across generations, but the essential form never changes. It is the Sphere’s preferred method of expressing force through mortal Will.

SKAU’RUN AND WILL ENCODING

The meaning behind the strike

Every strike carries faint Will Encoding—a lesser expression of the principle behind RULE. A Skau’Run attack is not simply physical impact; it bears the meaning of its Sphere.

FIRE

Ignition declared

The sword does not only cut and burn. It tells the world to ignite.

SPACE

Distance denied

The arrow does not merely pierce. It declares distance meaningless.

DEATH

Ending remembered

The scythe does not only wound. It reminds the target of finality.

STABLE DIVINE ARMAMENT

The Skau’Run

The bearer’s permanent personal weapon, teaching the soul how to hold divine authority and often acting as conduit, anchor or preparatory form.

COMPLETE RESONANCE

The RULE Lance

A temporary Grade 9 manifestation used to impose a reality-altering law. RULE is what happens when divine authority becomes absolute for one moment.

FINAL DOCTRINE

The shape of a divine role

A Skau’Run is the Sphere’s answer to one question:

If my Will must enter battle, what form shall it take?

For Fire, a blade. For Space, an arrow. For Death, a scythe. For Knowledge, a quill. For Fate, needles and thread.

Every Skau’Run is a weapon, but not every weapon is made to kill. Some bind. Some reveal. Some judge. Some remember.

And some end the world only when the hand that holds them finally dares.
